## Proposed customer copy

A compact set of short interface sounds for menus, buttons and feedback in games or interactive projects.

The pack is designed around clear functions rather than a large pile of loosely related effects. Choose a cue by its filename, audition it, and adjust the level to suit your interface.

Planned contents, to be verified against the final ZIP:

- 24 original procedural sound effects.
- Mono WAV, 48 kHz, 16-bit PCM.
- One audition sequence and a file/function guide.
- No plugin, subscription or network connection required to play the files.

These sounds were synthesized with original code using AI-assisted design and development. They are not microphone recordings, borrowed samples or imitations of a named creator. This is not a “No AI” product.

Listen to the preview before buying. The intended style is restrained electronic UI feedback, not cinematic impacts, voice, music or a universal audio solution. Set playback volume for your own project and audience.

Usage permissions will be stated in the approved license supplied with the pack. Support and refunds follow the applicable marketplace terms. No custom sound-design work, lifetime updates or compatibility with every engine is promised.
