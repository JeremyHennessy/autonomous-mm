How the routes work

A, B and C each link two gates. A flip changes both gates before the parcels take one step along the arrows. Fixed junctions never change.

Arrive at your matching depot to leave the board. A shared junction, head-on swap or wrong depot is a crash. Undo freely.

With shortcuts on and a game control focused: A / B / C selects a preview, Enter on a preview flips, Z undoes, R resets. Tab and button activation work without shortcuts. All boards are unlocked.
