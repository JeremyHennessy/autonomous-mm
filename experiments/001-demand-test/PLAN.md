# Experiment 001: reproducible CSV repair pilot

Decision date: 2026-10-22. Status: preparation, not launched. This is the first test, not a proven business.

## Hypothesis

A buyer with an explicit, bounded CSV cleanup need will pay USD 99 for an original-preserving transformation, exception/change report and reusable script. Delivery can be completed within two operator hours after agreed rules and valid input. Distribution and willingness to pay are the key unknowns.

## Offer and boundaries

- One UTF-8 CSV table, at most 10,000 data rows and 20 columns, subject to a sample-based fit check.
- At most three explicit, agreed transformation rules. Exact duplicate handling must be agreed; conflicting business-key records are flagged, never silently merged.
- Deliver cleaned CSV, documented exceptions/changes and a reusable Python script/configuration with instructions.
- Target delivery within 48 hours only after scope, permitted data handling, payment arrangements and capacity are confirmed. No deadline or contract has been accepted.
- One correction of a failure to follow agreed rules, not unlimited changes or new scope.
- No sensitive personal data, credentials, confidential customer lists, regulated decision-making, macros, fuzzy entity resolution, missing-fact reconstruction or live system access. Public-safe synthetic sample only during preparation.
- Material AI involvement disclosed; no claim of human review, certifications, prior customers or testimonials.
- USD 99 is a price hypothesis, not a market-cleared price. Fees, settlement, applicable tax, refunds and legal terms must be resolved before a transaction.

## Acquisition protocol

After specific channel/account/identity and messaging authorization: at most ten individually qualified responses to current explicit buyer requests through the requested, permitted channel. No bulk unsolicited campaign, paid bidding, automated account activity prohibited by a platform, or off-platform circumvention. Record eligibility evidence before each response. Public repo gets only aggregate stage counts and public source references; private correspondence and customer files stay out of git.

The initial search has not qualified a current buyer or free usable channel. See [observations](channel-observations.md). Do not mark this experiment launched until the first authorized offer is actually sent or published and the result is verified.

## Measures and decision

- Track inspected requests, eligible requests, sent offers, qualified responses, agreed pilots, invoiced amount, settled receipts, refunds/fees and operator time separately.
- Commercial success: at least one USD 99 pilot delivered under agreed criteria and receipt/fees reconciled within seven days of the first live eligible offer, subject to actual payout holds. A paid but unsettled balance remains pending, not verified settled profit.
- Review after ten eligible offers with no qualified response, or three qualified conversations with no willingness to pay USD 99. Investigate fit, trust and price; do not automatically spam more people or discount.
- If two deliveries exceed two operator hours, reduce scope or reprice before scaling. Do not hide acquisition, setup or repair time.
- Spending cap remains zero. No platform fees, account commitments or paid service activation without appropriate authorization. Stop on unauthorized data or actions, disputed rights or unsafe file content.

## 24-hour / 72-hour / 7-day / 30-day plan

These are intended milestones, not scheduled jobs or guarantees of unattended work.

1. **First 24 hours:** finish the synthetic demonstration and tests, preserve the offer/research/checkpoint in GitHub, request the smallest specific sales-channel and identity authorization. Verify receiver/payment arrangements before accepting any work. Do not build a storefront.
2. **By 72 hours, if access/permissions are supplied:** qualify and send up to ten permitted individual responses, recording actual outcomes. Scope a redacted sample for any interested buyer. No new spending or binding agreement assumed.
3. **By day 7 after launch:** deliver the first accepted pilot if one exists, reconcile actual settlement and costs, assess delivery time and objections. If no qualified buyer engages at the decision threshold, revise the offer/channel based on evidence rather than build more software.
4. **By day 30:** pursue repeatable delivery only if at least three paid, accepted pilots show positive contribution after measured direct costs and reasonable operator time. Consider recurring automation only for a demonstrated repeating task. Otherwise change the offer/model and record why. No number of sales is promised.

## Actual execution to date

Research and channel checks completed. Synthetic delivery demo is being prepared. No outreach, customer acceptance, payment, refund, production data processing or external account setup has occurred in this experiment. Realized net profit is not yet computable from verified financial evidence.
