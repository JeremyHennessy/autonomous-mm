# Acquisition observations

Observed 2026-10-22. Research only; no prospect has been contacted, no offer posted, and no customer acquired through this session.

## What has actually been checked

- Public search results for spreadsheet/data repair buyer requests returned predominantly competing supplier advertisements. These are evidence of supply, not paying demand. A free marketplace listing cannot be assumed to generate customers.
- Upwork's indexed data-cleansing category showed 231 jobs, but direct retrieval returned HTTP 403. The count is a search snapshot, not verified currently available suitable work. Do not retry that denied URL through another route.
- An indexed $100 Python Excel/CSV processing brief was dated September 22, 2026, with 50+ proposals and no interviews in the snapshot. It is historical demand evidence, not a qualified current lead or confirmed payment.
- An indexed WooCommerce brief described about 1,440 products across three supplier files and two multilingual stores. Direct retrieval returned HTTP 403. Current availability, buyer identity and detailed fit remain unverified. Do not submit a proposal from this snippet alone.
- Plugin discovery for Reddit, Upwork, Fiverr, PayPal and Stripe returned an eligible, uninstalled Stripe plugin and an unrelated broader analytics plugin. No marketplace or payment connection was established. Search is not an exhaustive inventory of every possible integration.

## Commercial guardrails

1. Qualify an actual buyer request, deadline, scope, accepted response channel and permission before treating it as a prospect.
2. Do not contact a marketplace buyer outside that marketplace or bypass paid proposal requirements.
3. Do not mistake supplier ads, views, replies or proposals for revenue.
4. First contact should request only a problem description and fictional/redacted example, not private datasets, passwords or account access.
5. Disclose material AI involvement. Do not imply human review, credentials, prior clients or testimonials that do not exist.
6. Commercial messages, identity use, account setup and binding service commitments remain subject to explicit authorization. No cold-email campaign has been authorized.
7. A response to an actual request has a different CASL analysis from an unsolicited promotion, but context and platform rules still matter. A published address alone is not blanket permission.
8. Payment-provider transaction fees, payout holds, refunds, FX and tax obligations prevent treating the entire invoice as profit. No receiver account or payment rail has been verified.
