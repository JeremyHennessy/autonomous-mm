# Portfolio checkpoint
2026-10-22. Public-safe summary of broader research and bounded local validation. No private analytics, customer data, account details, prototype implementation or paid binaries are included.

## What changed
The operating review reports a broader 22-model screen across overlooked digital products, distribution-first models, service opportunities and low-owner-effort tools. This is research breadth, not 22 validated opportunities or 22 product builds. Local recommendations changed as owner effort, acquisition and competitive evidence were considered.

- Services such as bounded layout repair had adjacent completed-order evidence, but no authenticated currently executable buyer request. Per-customer intake and delivery increase owner/operating effort; parked rather than opening another account.
- Maker workflow assets had stronger adjacent purchase signals than some software ideas, but physical validation and acquisition remained unresolved. Generic templates, subtitle repair and sprite tools faced strong free or cheap competition.
- Marketplace-distributed software, IDE plugins, data licensing and stock/public-domain products offer different discovery and fulfillment mechanisms. None establishes demand for this operator. [Apify monetization](https://docs.apify.com/actors/publishing/monetize), [JetBrains publisher economics](https://plugins.jetbrains.com/docs/marketplace/revenue-sharing-and-fees.html) and [Etsy fees](https://www.etsy.com/legal/fees) show why channel gates and costs cannot be treated as interchangeable or universally free.

## Live primary experiment
[Quiet Interface](https://autonomous-mm.itch.io/quiet-interface) is the sole live product, with an approved public announcement already recorded. Audio/buttons/UI tag improvements were confirmed; accurate required AI classification remains. Publication and fulfillment mechanisms are not demand or profit. No private account analytics are disclosed here. Continue qualified acquisition within channel rules and granted permissions rather than making another asset pack.

## Migration-loss checking: preferred next evidence gate
A local feasibility candidate received 34 passing tests and independent review. An authentic, MIT-licensed upstream fixture covers four Notion HTML pages paired with recorded Markdown. The check identified 55 selected supported text blocks through title-based page matching. This is narrow evidence, not full preservation or native-application compatibility.

Excluded or unresolved coverage includes duplicate/renamed titles, formatting and punctuation, block order/multiplicity, unsupported nested content and links, and binary attachment preservation. Zero findings within selected coverage does not mean zero migration loss. Synthetic mutation checks are not observed customer failures. No real customer notes were used. A local demo is prepared, not browser-verified.

Primary sources: [upstream test fixture](https://github.com/obsidianmd/obsidian-importer/blob/master/tests/notion/notion-testspace.zip), [upstream MIT license](https://github.com/obsidianmd/obsidian-importer/blob/master/LICENSE), [official Notion import guide](https://help.obsidian.md/import/notion). The [import reporting request](https://github.com/obsidianmd/obsidian-importer/issues/580) was implemented, so generic import logging is not a novel gap. The [large-import report](https://github.com/obsidianmd/obsidian-importer/issues/561) is closed, not evidence of a currently unresolved engine defect.

Next gate: authentic buyer interest in independent source/output checking, representative native conversion evidence and honest coverage/false-positive review. No broad compatibility or paid readiness claim; do not expand parser scope simply to accumulate features.

## OBS scene-handoff preflight: parked technical candidate
A separate local candidate received 39 passing tests and independent review. Evidence is synthetic only: actual OBS compatibility is not established. It is not a comprehensive secret detector or a guarantee that a scene is safe to publish. No native-app or customer workflow success is claimed.

Primary sources: [OBS portable-mode guidance](https://obsproject.com/kb/portable-mode) documents relocation limitations; [Scene Collection Manager](https://obsproject.com/forum/resources/scene-collection-manager.1434/) and [Elgato scene-collection tooling](https://docs.elgato.com/obs/scene-collections/) are substantive existing alternatives. Download counts or feature lists are not paid-customer evidence.

Next gate: native OBS compatibility plus a specific buyer workflow not already handled adequately by established tools. Keep parked while the migration gate is assessed; no second product launch or polished commercial UI is justified by synthetic tests.

## Boundaries and decision
One primary live experiment; at most one prioritized low-cost independent evidence test. Test counts are reported local engineering results, not remotely reproducible public product releases or revenue validation. Private source and detailed commercial implementation stay outside this repository. No new account, outreach, paid promotion, spending, public prototype deployment or scheduler is authorized by this checkpoint. Static delivery and local demos do not create an autonomous reasoning runtime.
