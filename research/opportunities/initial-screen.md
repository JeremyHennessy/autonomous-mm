# Initial opportunity screen

As of: 2026-10-22 UTC. Independent blank-slate initiative; no other owner projects, relationships, customers, or expertise assumed. Research only: no accounts, outreach, submissions, purchases, testing of third-party systems, or deployment performed. AGENTS.md and README.md were read through the GitHub connector before research.

## Decision

Choose a **fixed-scope, reproducible CSV cleanup service**, initially a USD 99 pilot: one non-sensitive CSV, up to 10,000 rows and 20 columns, agreed normalization rules, exact-duplicate reporting, cleaned copy, change report, and a reusable local script. No fuzzy merging, financial/health advice, scraping, or production writes. Propose 48-hour delivery only after validating sample, scope, authorized processing environment, and availability. Do not promise accurate reconstruction of missing facts.

Why: an identifiable short task can be demonstrated before building a product; a recent buyer request specifically asks for a reusable Python cleaning script at $100. A 2026 platform report analyzes completed paid jobs, rather than merely seller aspirations. The choice is about testability and short fulfillment, not an assumption about the owner's background. This is **not validated demand for our offer**. Generic cleanup is intensely commoditized; current buyer postings include $10, $25 and $75 budgets. The differentiator to test is transparent, repeatable transformations rather than cheap manual entry.

Biggest invalidating assumption: reaching suitable buyers through a permitted genuinely free channel and converting at $99 without an established reputation. No such channel/account or conversion has been verified. If that cannot be unlocked, the revenue experiment is blocked, not running.

Cash downside: zero new discretionary spending is authorized. Economic downside still includes research/owner time. ## Smallest next experiment

Prepare one synthetic before/after sample and test the local transformation script. No customer data or personalized contact lists in this public repo. Prepare one truthful $99 offer, with no invented credentials/testimonials, and a short intake specifying acceptable non-sensitive data and the customer's right to share it. Do not build a SaaS, buy a domain, or deploy inference.

Before outreach, obtain bounded permission for exact channel/identity/audience and confirm a compliant zero-upfront-cost route. First proposed batch: at most 10 individually screened responses to explicit recent buyer requests, no bulk cold messages, no paid Connects, no off-platform circumvention. Availability, scope, legitimacy and geography must be reverified immediately before each contact. Separately obtain authorization and privacy-safe setup for any payment account/legal commitments. No credentials in public records.

Falsifiable success: within seven days of an approved offer actually reaching suitable buyers, obtain at least one $99 paid pilot, successfully deliver, and reconcile settlement/fees/refunds; record qualified conversations separately. Stop/revise after 10 eligible offers without a qualified response, or three qualified conversations without willingness to pay $99. This is a learning boundary, not an automatic recurring outreach authorization. If delivery exceeds two hours on two pilots, reduce scope or reprice before scaling. No automatic price discount or extra promise.

Research is capped at this initial screen. The next valuable action is a demonstrable delivery sample and authorized real buyer test, not more speculative opportunity rankings.
