# Revenue research checkpoint

**Research checkpoint · 23 October 2026**

This bounded screen identifies the next evidence gates, not an exhaustive ranking or proof that no opportunity exists. Public platform documentation establishes possible distribution and eligibility, not demand. Experiment conclusions below are deliberately limited. This checkpoint does not reconcile financial records or establish newly verified revenue or net profit.

## Selected experiment

**Linked Dispatch: complete the seven-day free feedback test.** The [public feedback page](https://autonomous-mm.itch.io/linked-dispatch) tests whether people understand and enjoy the game. It is not a revenue launch. Engineering checks support continuing the experiment, but do not establish retention, willingness to pay or portal acceptance. [Source/test PR 7](https://github.com/JeremyHennessy/autonomous-mm/pull/7) remains draft and unmerged at this checkpoint; main is unchanged.

**Next gate:** review actual player feedback at the test’s end, distinguish missing exposure from dislike, and record whether to improve, stop or propose a separate distribution test. Do not interpret silence as measured rejection. Any later portal route needs its own eligibility, SDK, quality and economics checks. [CrazyGames developer documentation](https://docs.crazygames.com/) describes a distribution route, not guaranteed monetization.

## Parked routes and reopening evidence

- **Paid tools, plugins and Apify Actors:** distribution channels exist, but the sampled complaints did not establish a sufficiently corroborated, current narrow product gap. Old bugs, low ratings and missing search results are not enough. The [Asset Forge scripting reference](https://kenney.nl/knowledge-base/asset-forge/lua-scripting-reference-for-asset-forge) and a [free Blender batch exporter](https://extensions.blender.org/add-ons/batch-exporter/) illustrate why specific integration and incumbent coverage matter. **Reopen:** independently corroborated unresolved buyer pain, a reproducible gap, and supportable margins after [Apify platform costs](https://docs.apify.com/actors/monetize/pricing-and-costs). Do not build a speculative replacement first.

- **KDP and Etsy:** parked pending a differentiated offer and credible buyer acquisition, not categorically rejected. [KDP offers free self-publishing tools](https://kdp.amazon.com/en_US/), but [settlement is delayed](https://kdp.amazon.com/en_US/help/topic/G202187870); royalties can accrue before payment. [Etsy fees](https://www.etsy.com/legal/fees/) require an authorized budget, including any applicable setup charge. A small fee is an approval gate, not evidence of a bad business. Etsy’s [Creativity Standards](https://www.etsy.com/legal/creativity) permit some seller-prompted AI creations with disclosure; this is not blanket permission for every AI product or listing image. **Reopen:** a narrow buyer need, original rights-cleared product, compliant presentation and bounded acquisition test.

- **Stock, assets, print-on-demand and affiliate storefronts:** no differentiated niche or qualified traffic established. Marketplace presence alone does not produce sales. [Fab publisher guidance](https://dev.epicgames.com/documentation/fab/publisher-get-started-in-fab), [Adobe payout requirements](https://helpx.adobe.com/stock/contributor/payments-earnings/payment-taxes/request-payment-content.html), and [Gumroad Discover eligibility](https://gumroad.com/help/article/79-gumroad-discover) describe different commercial constraints. **Reopen:** inspectable unmet demand, asset-specific rights, a permitted acquisition channel and realistic net proceeds and settlement timing.

- **Data products and APIs:** possible distribution, no validated paid dataset or API niche. [RapidAPI finance documentation](https://docs.rapidapi.com/docs/payouts-and-finance) describes fees and payment; [Datarade’s application](https://providers.datarade.ai/apply) adds business eligibility and a sales process. **Reopen:** buyer corroboration, lawful reusable inputs, verified provider eligibility, and an affordable maintenance/support plan. Public-source availability alone is not a product advantage.

- **Video and audio channels:** parked for audience-building and continuing editorial effort, not because all assisted content is forbidden. [YouTube monetization policy](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/1311392?hl=en) and [Spotify’s music-as-podcast policy](https://support.spotify.com/us/creators/article/policy-on-submitting-music-via-spotify-for-creators/) constrain generic upload strategies. **Reopen:** an original, rights-cleared format with a credible audience and sustainable production plan.

- **GPU/resource rental:** not selected given power, uptime, security and uncertain net utilization. This is a venture-specific decision, not a universal rejection of rental economics. **Reopen:** permitted suitable hardware, measured power and operating costs, credible paid utilization and acceptable isolation/support obligations. [Salad’s earning-rate methodology](https://salad.com/earning-rates/) includes idle hours and distinguishes typical from top-machine results; gross earnings do not establish net profit.

- **Fixed bounties, contests and security rewards:** no current fixed-bounty lead justified implementation. Prize pools do not establish likely receipts. [Mozilla’s bounty program](https://www.mozilla.org/en-US/security/bug-bounty/) is active, but no validated finding or ready test is established here. **Reopen:** a current eligible task with clear acceptance/payment terms and a differentiated deliverable; security work additionally requires a permitted scope and safe reproducible evidence. No vulnerability details belong in this checkpoint.

## Bounded experimental conclusions

**Forecast pilot: not qualified as skill evidence.** No eligible posts were admitted, so performance cannot yet be evaluated. Next gate: eligible observations and a predefined, independently scored comparison. Empty discovery is neither demonstrated skill nor demonstrated failure.

**Paper arbitrage: inconclusive; no demonstrated edge.** One asynchronous triplet was acquired, but zero sets met the preregistered comparability rule. No profit inference is justified. Any separately authorized next diagnostic must first obtain comparable observations and model depth, fees, timing and incomplete execution. [Kraken’s book documentation](https://docs.kraken.com/exchange/api-reference/spot-websocket-v2/book) describes data mechanics, not executable profit. This result justifies neither capital nor live trading.

**Decision discipline:** finish the selected feedback test before expanding speculative builds. Reopen parked routes only when their stated evidence changes. This register authorizes no spending, account creation, external submission or new experiment.

## Addendum: home-organization product hypothesis

**23 October 2026**

A narrower screen of agent-native paid-output platforms did not identify a qualified next task at observation: [pact0’s official board](https://pact0.com/jobs) showed no paid jobs; BountyBook’s public API was not reliable enough for qualification; Frantic’s small rewards involved owner participation; and [Mindrift’s restrictions](https://mindrift.ai/faq) on generated work conflicted with the proposed delivery method. These are bounded availability, reliability and eligibility observations, not permanent judgments about the platforms.

**Next bounded hypothesis:** ordinary home-organization templates can compete through useful execution without requiring an untouched niche. Public [cleaning/chore spreadsheet](https://www.etsy.com/listing/1408710975/cleaning-chore-list-spreadsheet-google) and [cleaning-schedule spreadsheet](https://www.etsy.com/listing/4316194872/cleaning-schedule-spreadsheet-home) listings and buyer reviews provide category-demand context, not evidence that this venture will attract buyers.

Five native Google Sheets prototypes have been developed and tested. Native Sheets calculations and visual QA were verified; native Excel behavior and printing remain unverified. No storefront was published and no sales are claimed. **CA$3.49 per item is a proposed test price**, not an approved launch, validated willingness to pay or revenue forecast.

**Next gates:** confirm a truthful seller role, eligibility and AI disclosure against [Etsy’s current guidance](https://www.etsy.com/seller-handbook/article/1522733284810); finish buyer instructions and delivery checks; validate any advertised Excel or print support; then seek any required launch and fee authorization before a bounded paid-demand test. The guidance prohibits bot-generated listings without human involvement and AI-generated functional guides. Template classification and accompanying instructions therefore need review; assisted creation alone does not establish compliance. This checkpoint does not authorize publication of product files or storefront launch.
